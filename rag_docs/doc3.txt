The model is served two ways: as a BentoML service for fast local iteration, and as
a KServe InferenceService on Kubernetes for production, with minReplicas set to 0 to
enable scale-to-zero. Features are managed through Feast, with an offline store for
training and an online SQLite store for real-time serving, achieving lookup latency
of under 5 milliseconds per request. Discount offers from the retention agent are
capped at 25 percent maximum, enforced in code regardless of what is requested.
